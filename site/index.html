UAT POWER-UP SETTINGS

Set each power-up spawn weight from 0% (off) to 300%. Higher values make that power-up more likely relative to the others. Changes are saved on this device.

Controls: ← → move · ↑ ↓ also work · P or Esc to pause/resume. Green shields grant 20 seconds of invincibility. Blue magnets pull coins towards you for 10 seconds; orange converters turn asteroids into coins for 8 seconds. Pink high-value coins are worth five coins. After a hit, your ship flashes for 2 seconds.
